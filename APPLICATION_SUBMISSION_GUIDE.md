# Application Submission Guide

## 📋 How to Submit Your Application

You have **two convenient options** to submit your resume/requirements for the job application. Choose whichever works best for you!

---

## Option 1: 📄 Upload PDF File

### When to use PDF upload:
- You have your resume/requirements as a PDF file on your computer
- You want the fastest submission method
- You prefer keeping a local backup

### How to submit with PDF:

1. **Select "📄 PDF File"** option at the top
2. **Click the upload area** or drag & drop your PDF file
3. **Verify your file** appears (you'll see the filename)
4. **Add notes (optional)** - Write any additional information you want to share
5. **Click "Submit Application"** button

### PDF Requirements:
- ✅ **Format**: Must be PDF (.pdf file)
- ✅ **Size**: Maximum 50MB (recommended under 20MB for mobile)
- ✅ **Mobile tip**: For faster upload on mobile, keep files under 5MB if possible

### What happens after PDF upload:
- Your file is securely stored on our servers
- You can view your PDF anytime in "My Applications"
- HR team can view your PDF with one click
- Your submission is instantly confirmed

---

## Option 2: 🔗 Google Drive Link

### When to use Google Drive link:
- You already have your resume/requirements in Google Drive
- You want instant submission (no file upload needed)
- You prefer to keep your documents in Google Drive for easy updates
- You want to share a folder or document directly

### How to submit with Google Drive:

1. **Select "🔗 Google Drive Link"** option at the top
2. **Copy your Google Drive link**:
   - Right-click your file/folder in Google Drive
   - Click "Share"
   - Set access to "Anyone with the link can view"
   - Copy the link
3. **Paste the link** in the text field
4. **Add notes (optional)** - Write any additional information
5. **Click "Submit Application"** button

### Google Drive Link Support:
- ✅ **Individual files**: https://drive.google.com/file/d/...
- ✅ **Folders**: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/...
- ✅ **Google Docs**: https://docs.google.com/document/d/...
- ✅ **Google Sheets**: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/...
- ✅ **Google Slides**: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/...

### Important - Sharing Permission:
⚠️ **Make sure your Google Drive file/folder is accessible!**
- Set sharing to **"Viewer"** access (read-only recommended)
- Change access from "Restricted" to **"Anyone with the link can view"**
- Without proper sharing, HR cannot access your document

### What happens after Google Drive submission:
- Your link is saved instantly (no upload delay)
- HR team can click the link and view your document directly in Google Drive
- You can still update your document in Google Drive anytime
- Your submission is instantly confirmed

## ✅ After Submission

### You'll see:
- ✅ Success message with submission confirmation
- ✅ Your application appears in "My Applications"
- ✅ Application status shows "Under Review"

### You can:
- 📝 **Edit** (if status is "Under Review")
  - Update your PDF or notes
  - Change your Google Drive link
- 👀 **View** your submitted document anytime
- 📧 Receive updates when HR reviews your application

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### PDF Upload Issues:
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "File too large" | Use a smaller PDF (under 20MB recommended) |
| "Upload stuck" | Check internet connection, try again |
| "Format not supported" | Ensure file is PDF (.pdf) |

### Google Drive Link Issues:
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Link not accessible" | Verify sharing is set to "Anyone with link can view" |
| "Invalid link" | Make sure you copied the full link |
| "Access denied" | Check that you shared the correct file/folder |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **For PDF**: Optimize your PDF size by compressing images before uploading
2. **For Google Drive**: Use a shared folder to organize all your application documents
3. **Always verify**: Check that your submitted file/link is correct and readable
4. **Mobile friendly**: Both methods work great on mobile and desktop
5. **Keep records**: Save confirmation emails or screenshots of your submissions
